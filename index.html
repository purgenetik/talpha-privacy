TAlpha Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

TAlpha is an application for calculating alpha-decay half-lives for scientific, educational, and research purposes.

Data entered into TAlpha

TAlpha does not collect, store, or transmit the nuclear calculation data entered by the user.

Input parameters and calculation results are processed locally on the user's device and are not transmitted by TAlpha.

Advertising

The free version of TAlpha uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may process device, advertising, diagnostic, and usage information in accordance with Google's privacy policies.

Premium purchases

TAlpha uses Google Play Billing to process purchases of TAlpha Premium. Purchase transactions and payment information are handled by Google Play. TAlpha does not collect or store users' payment card or banking information.

Children

TAlpha is a scientific and educational calculation tool and is not specifically designed for children.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the functionality of TAlpha or the third-party services used by the application change. The current version will be published on this page.
