Premium Features

Premium entitlement

TAlpha uses Google Play Billing for its Premium in-app product.

The v5.3 documentation target uses Premium state to gate or alter several features.

Premium features in v5.3

  • removal of advertising;
  • calculation-history sharing;
  • Chart of α-decaying nuclei;
  • personal logo;
  • Premium launcher presentation.

Chart gating

The navigation drawer always shows the Chart entry, but it is visually disabled/marked Premium for non-Premium users.

Selecting it without Premium opens the Premium dialog instead of the chart.

Personal logo

Premium users can install a personal logo. The image is stored locally as:

personal_logo.png

In v5.3 the personal logo is displayed in the center of the fixed footer, while the standard TAlpha application logo remains in the upper-right header position.

Billing

The application:

  1. creates a Billing client;
  2. connects to Google Play;
  3. queries Premium product information;
  4. queries purchases;
  5. launches a purchase flow;
  6. processes purchase updates;
  7. updates application entitlement state.

Payment credentials are handled by Google Play rather than TAlpha.

Testing warning

Premium behavior should be tested through the appropriate Google Play testing/license-testing configuration. Local debug installation alone may not reproduce production purchase behavior.
